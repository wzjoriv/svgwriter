Demos¶

The demos/ folder holds complete design projects. Each one is an ordinary project, as svgwriter new makes them: svgwriter.toml, main.py, theme.toml, components/, animations/ and assets/, and each uses several features together. Open a demo’s folder and run svgwriter preview to see it live (with its controls, where it has them), or svgwriter build to write its outputs into output/. The examples/ stay short scripts about one topic each; see Projects for how projects work.

Every demo also has a Jupyter notebook beside its main.py, named after the demo (<name>/<name>.ipynb), that shows the code cell by cell with pictures, a panel for the demo’s controls when run, and stills for other values. python scripts/dev.py svgwriter demo-notebooks build writes them.

Playing card¶

demos/playing-card: a card with rank and suit buttons, hue, iridescence, glow, corner radius and width controls (@svg.widgets.controls), corner indices, pips and court panels built from layout groups, and a sweep of the same parameters written as a GIF. Outputs: SVG, PNG, PDF and GIF. Notebook.

Calculator kit¶

demos/calculator-kit: a calculator UI from VStack, HStack and Grid, with controls for the shell style, key shape, keypad layout, display font, size and alignment, history line, key font and weight, spacing and palette. Outputs: SVG, PNG and PDF. Notebook.

Effects¶

demos/effects: a passage on a night card where five words carry the smoke, metal, wind, fire and snow effects while staying text, beside a logo and two buttons with effects of their own, and a dropdown per word, for the logo and for the buttons to swap each effect. Outputs: an animated SVG and a GIF. Notebook.

Planets explainer¶

demos/planets-explainer: two scenes, the eight planets on orbits drawn to scale and Betelgeuse beside the Sun with a rolling counter, moved by behaviours, SMIL and per-frame updates, then baked; a time scale and a choice of scene drive them in the notebook. Outputs: MP4 (GIF without ffmpeg), animated SVG and PowerPoint. Notebook.

Blobs¶

demos/blobs: a website hero banner: a headline and a “Get started” button (hover and click in the SVG) beside metaballs that merge and split under the goo filter, moved by a project behaviour on a seamless loop, with controls for the number of blobs, their gooeyness, the speed, the headline and the button text. Outputs: GIF and animated SVG. Notebook.

Poster¶

demos/poster: an A4 print poster drawn in millimetres: an invitation to a beach clean-up with gradients, patterns, a route with markers, drawn icons of what to bring and a schedule read from CSV, and controls for the theme, an accent hue shift and the event’s title, date, time and place. Outputs: a true-size PDF, a 300 dpi PNG and an SVG. Notebook.

The pictures on this page are built from the demos by python scripts/dev.py svgwriter gallery.
